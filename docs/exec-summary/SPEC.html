Billing Record

Record income and expenses from a photo of a bill or bank slip.

Draft, awaiting approval · 2026-10-14

The goal

A personal web app for one user (you) to record income and expenses without typing them in by hand.

You upload a bank transfer slip, or photograph a bill or receipt with your phone. The app reads the image and pulls out the amount, currency, date, who was paid or who paid you, whether it is income or an expense, and a category.

You check the result, fix anything wrong, and save it. The app then adds up your records and shows totals as charts.

Slips and bills can be in Thai or English, and amounts can be in more than one currency.

Rules the AI follows

Always

  • Run the code checks and automatic tests before every commit.
  • Check all input and all AI output before it touches the database.
  • Keep the API key in a private settings file and use it on the server only.
  • Store money as whole numbers of the smallest unit (such as satang or cents).
  • Check for secrets and personal data before every push, because the repo is public.

Asks you first

  • Adding a software package that is not listed in the spec.
  • Changing the database layout after the first version.
  • Running anything that makes real AI calls, because it costs credits.
  • Changing the AI model or the instructions given to it in ways that affect cost.
  • Making the app reachable beyond the home network in Phase 1.
  • Every Railway action: creating the service or volume, setting variables, deploying and moving data.
  • Anything that adds Railway cost.

Never

  • Commit private settings, the database, uploaded images, scratch files or handoff notes.
  • Commit real slips, receipts or any personal financial data, even as test files.
  • Send images anywhere other than the AI service. The exchange rate service gets only a date and a currency code.
  • Save an AI-read record without your confirmation.
  • Delete or skip failing tests to get to green.
  • Put the app on the public internet without the login in place and tested.
  • Run more than one copy of the app against the database volume.

Phase 1: Local

Build every feature in the spec and run it on your PC, with access from your phone over the home network.

No login. Data lives in a data folder on your PC.

Done when Phase 1 success criteria 1-8 pass.

Phase 2: Deploy to Railway

Put the same app on your existing Railway account as a public web app with HTTPS, so it works from your phone anywhere. Data lives on a Railway volume.

How you use it in Phase 1

The app runs on your PC. To use the phone camera, the phone opens the app over the home network.

The app has no login, so anyone on the home network can open it. Remote access is out of scope for v1.

Internet is needed to read images and get exchange rates. Adding THB records by hand works offline.

Source: SPEC.md > Runtime and access (Phase 1)

Phase 2 on Railway

Railway builds and runs the app from the GitHub project, gives it an HTTPS address, and keeps a volume so records and images survive redeploys.

Login: one password, no usernames. A successful login lasts 30 days. After 5 failed tries in 15 minutes, login is blocked for 15 minutes. A logout button ends the session.

Moving data: a one-time script copies your Phase 1 records and images to Railway, then record counts and totals are compared.

Backup: the CSV export stays the main backup. Railway volume backups are turned on if your plan includes them.

Cost: Railway bills by usage. One small always-on service with a small volume is expected to stay within the entry plan. You confirm this before deploying.

Source: SPEC.md > Phase 2 Deployment (Railway)

Look and feel

Clean, quiet and practical, and not AI-looking: neutral colours, one accent colour, no gradients or emoji.

Income and expense use a muted green and red, always with a sign or label.

Amounts use aligned numerals. Charts use at most 6 category colours, with small ones grouped into Other.

The Add record button is always one tap away on mobile. Every screen has empty, loading and error states.

Readable contrast, full keyboard use and labelled form fields.

Source: SPEC.md > Design Direction

How it will be tested

Automatic checks cover the sums, money and date conversion, AI result reading, duplicate check, CSV output, saving and editing records, and a full walk through the app on desktop and phone sizes.

Real AI calls are not part of the automatic checks. One separate script runs a single real read by hand, because it costs credits.

Test files use made-up or fully blanked-out slips only. Never real account numbers or names.

Source: SPEC.md > Testing Strategy

Rules for categories, money and duplicates

Default expense categories: Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills and Utilities, Health, Entertainment, Housing, Transfer out, Other. Default income categories: Salary, Transfer in, Refund, Other income.

You can add, rename and remove categories. A category in use cannot be removed until its records are moved.

Each record keeps its original amount and currency, the exchange rate to THB and the THB amount. For other currencies the app fetches the rate for the record's date. You can override it. If it cannot be fetched, the form asks for a rate and will not save without one.

If a new record has the same amount, date and slip reference number as an existing one, the app warns before saving.

The pages work on a phone screen 360 px wide and on desktop.
